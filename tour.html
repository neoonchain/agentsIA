Trade with the market

Run 9 only buys dips while BTC is above its 200-day average: in 2022 that kept it out of the crash completely, while run 10, which trends faster, lost 37%.

Two edges beat one

Dip buying wins often and small; trend following loses often and wins big. Half and half, the worst drop over the last two years fell from 37% to 24%.

Volume or nothing

Run 1's loss was a wide candle on weekend volume (0.5×). In run 2, the same filter skipped a false ETH breakdown that would have lost.

Fees change the math

0.4% each side took 39% of the planned risk on a 2.1% stop, so a 1R stop really cost 1.39R. The fix: stops of 3% or more and targets of 2.5R or more.

Decide often, never chase

Deciding every 20h missed a clean BTC breakdown. Run 2 decided every 12h, and the app checks every 60 minutes by default.

Too strict misses winners

Runs 2 and 3 avoided every trap but made no trades, and zero trades means zero progress toward the marks. Run 4's rule: when a setup meets the rules, take it, without stacking extra conditions on top.

Crashes come in steps

The 8 Oct memecoin crash fell about 4% over 25 minutes and never 3% in any 3 minutes, so a 3-minute alarm would have stayed silent. The shock detector now also watches 15-minute windows.

The gate works

Run 5 lost on months it had never seen, and Brake stopped it twice at the 8% drawdown limit. That's the point: a strategy that can't beat the marks on paper never gets near your money.

Fees decide it

Before fees, the first half was profitable (profit factor 1.68). Kraken-level fees of 0.4% each side took $295 and turned it into a loss. Breakouts with 3% stops need cheaper fills (maker orders) or fewer, bigger moves.

Cheaper fills aren't an edge

Limit orders cut the fees by about a third, but run 6 still ended with a profit factor of 1.09 on its unseen year. Saving on costs helps a strategy that already works; it can't create one.

Your limit, your call

Trend-following had a great 2024, yet every year 4 coins that move together dragged it to the 8% drawdown limit. A looser limit means bigger losses with real money, so only you can change it: run 8 tests 12%.

More room isn't an edge

At 12% the strategy simply lost more before Brake stopped it: three of four years still ended in a loss. The limit was doing its job; the strategy needs a better entry, not a looser leash.

Fifty trades is a high bar

No run reached 50 trades in a year without losing. Slow strategies trade too little; fast ones pay too much in fees. That's why the marks ask for it.

Don't strangle winners

Run 4 picked two good breakouts and still lost $14.90, because both stops were pulled up under the latest small dip. Left alone, they would have made about +$200. The new rule: only move a stop under a real swing low with room for normal noise, and not before +1.5R.

Runs 1 to 4 are a handful of AI decisions and prove nothing statistically; runs 5 to 10 test exact rules over whole years. Either way, the marks decide: no strategy gets real money before 50 paper trades over 30 days, a profit factor of 1.5 or more and a drawdown under your limit (25% now that you raised it for the portfolio; 12% until run 9, 8% until run 7). A profit factor is only shown once a strategy has both wins and losses, and a win rate only after 10 trades.

(10)ARGUS · FAST NEWS

Argus sees the news in seconds.

Argus, named after the giant with a hundred eyes who never slept, is the watcher inside Kairos. Every 30 seconds it reads the fastest free crypto feeds and the 1-minute candles of every coin your agents watch. A big move on heavy volume makes running strategies decide right away, and Brake blocks new entries on that coin for 15 minutes so nobody chases the candle.

02 · SHOCKS

Price, not just headlines

A move past 4× the coin's normal volatility in 3 minutes, or a slower cascade over 15 minutes on 2.5× volume. Often the price moves before any headline exists.

03 · AGENTS

Decide now, don't chase

Running strategies on that coin decide immediately, with the headlines and the shock in their prompt. Brake's shock guard blocks new entries for 15 minutes, unless a strategy is built for momentum.

04 · YOU

Bring your own tracker

Anything that can call a URL can feed the agents: j7tracker alerts forwarded through Telegram or Discord, TradingView alerts, n8n or Zapier. Each request needs your private token.

TESTED · 8 OCT 15:00 UTC

Replayed on real 1-minute candles, the detector flagged DOGE, PEPE and WIF 20 to 23 minutes into a −4% cascade, and they fell another 3 to 5.5% after the alert. No tool reliably sees a crash before it starts. The realistic goal is to know within seconds, and to stop the agents from chasing the candle.
