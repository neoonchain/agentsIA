(04)PLAN RULES

Rules you set once, and the contract keeps.

Each rule below is code in the planbook, not a setting on this site. The same nine hold for every plan and every wallet, and nobody can change them after deployment.

01

A deadline that really closes

Every plan carries the moment it ends, at most thirty days out. After that the planbook refuses every remaining step, even one you sign yourself.

UP TO 30 DAYSCHECKED PER STEP
02

An ETH ceiling you choose

You set the most ETH the whole plan may send. Each ETH step adds to a running total, and a step that would pass the ceiling reverts.

SET AT REVIEWRUNNING TOTAL
03

Recipients fixed at review

Each step commits its recipient, token and amount as a hash. Change any of them afterwards and the step stops matching, so it cannot run.

EXACT ADDRESSEXACT AMOUNT
04

One step, one signature

Nothing runs in a batch behind your back. Every step is a separate transaction that your wallet shows you and you approve.

YOUR WALLETEACH STEP
05

Steps that cannot repeat

When a step runs, its bit in the plan is set for good. Sending the same call again is refused, so a double click never pays twice.

RUN ONCE16 STEPS MAX

(05)YOUR CONTROL

What the agents can never do.

Automation usually means handing over a key. Here the agents do the reading and the drafting, and the part that moves money stays with you, one signature per step.

A PLAN'S STEPS, IN ORDERSTEP 05 WAITING FOR YOUR SIGNATURE

01

Agents propose, your wallet decides

The four agents run as code in your browser tab. They write a plan and explain it; they have no key and no way to send a transaction. Approval always happens in your wallet.

02

Exact approvals, never open-ended

A token step asks your wallet to approve the planbook for exactly that step's amount. There is no unlimited allowance waiting to be used later.

03

Reading is free and harmless

Balances, recipients and prices are read from the chain without a signature. Scout can look as often as you like and still cannot move a coin.

04

No custody, no keys on our side

ETH is forwarded in the same transaction and tokens move straight from you to the recipient. The contract keeps nothing, and this site stores no key.

(06)HOW A PLAN RUNS

From a sentence to a record.

Four stages, the same for a single payment or a sixteen-step run.

01WRITE

Say what you want

Type a goal in plain words: who gets what, in which asset, and by when. Add a spending ceiling if you want one; otherwise the plan's own ETH total becomes the cap.

02PLAN

Agents draft the steps

Atlas turns the goal into numbered steps, Scout reads balances and recipients, and Sentry lists every check it ran, with blocks that stop the plan outright.

03ONE TRANSACTION

Open the reviewed plan

Your wallet opens the plan in one transaction: its hash, deadline, ETH cap and one hash per step. Before the planbook is deployed, this is a signed message instead.

04EACH STEP

Sign, run, record

Run the steps in your order, each with its own signature. Ledger keeps the activity log with transaction links until the plan is done, closed or expired.
